# Business Overview

## What we are

A **sales talent placement and management agency** for remote closers and setters.

We are not a job board, not a recruiter, and not a coaching program. We are the
sales department a business rents instead of builds. A business owner comes to us
with an offer; we install proven reps onto that offer and keep managing them
after placement.

## The problem we replace

The status quo for an offer owner who needs sales reps:

1. Post in Facebook groups / job boards, get flooded with unqualified applicants
2. Screen resumes, run interviews, run mock calls, check references
3. Pick someone unproven and hope
4. Spend a week+ onboarding them — writing an SOP, explaining the offer,
   running practice calls before they touch a real lead
5. Discover they can't close, and start the entire cycle over

That is weeks of the owner's time, per rep, with no guarantee at the end.

## What we do instead

**A bench, not a search.** We maintain a roster of hundreds of pre-vetted closers
and setters. The vetting already happened. The owner picks from proven reps
instead of screening strangers.

**Same-day install.** Once we know the offer, we hand the rep a complete SOP and
offer rundown — what the product is, who the buyer is, the objections, the pitch,
the process. They understand the offer the day they're placed and are taking
calls the next day. No onboarding week.

**Ongoing internal coaching.** Placement is the start, not the deliverable. We
coach our reps continuously — mock calls, call review, tactical advice — so
close rate and booking rate improve month over month. The owner gets a rep who is
better in month three than in month one.

**Data and tracking software.** We give reps and the business the tooling to
track performance, so improvement is measured, not claimed, and the owner can see
what their sales are actually doing.

**Swap on demand.** If a rep isn't the right fit for the offer, the owner asks and
we swap them. The replacement is already qualified and can be onboarded within a
day — because we're pulling from the same bench, not restarting a hiring process.

## Pricing model

**A one-time access fee. No percentage of closed deals.**

- **$1,000 once** for lifetime access to the entire bench
- ClosingKing takes **0%** of closed revenue
- The closer takes their standard **~10%**, paid direct by the client

The fee covers the SOP build, onboarding, our ongoing coaching of the rep, and
unlimited swaps. Compared with a percentage model, the client keeps far more
margin per deal and we never sit between them and their own revenue.

## Core positioning

We are aligned with the client by structure, not by promise. Our incentive is
identical to theirs: more closed revenue. That is why we coach after placement
instead of collecting a placement fee and disappearing.

## Message hierarchy (most to least important)

1. **One fee, then nothing.** $1,000 once. We take 0% of your deals.
2. **Speed.** Placed today, closing tomorrow. Not a week of onboarding.
3. **Pre-vetted bench.** Hundreds of proven reps, not a pile of applicants.
4. **Managed, not just placed.** Ongoing coaching, mocks, and data — rep
   performance climbs month over month.
5. **Swap freedom.** Wrong fit? New qualified rep within a day.

## Who we sell to

Offer owners with a working offer and lead flow who need sales capacity —
coaches, consultants, agencies, info/education businesses, and any company
running a high-ticket phone-closed offer.

## Two audiences to keep distinct

- **Businesses** (primary buyer): want revenue without hiring risk
- **Closers and setters** (supply side): want placement onto good offers plus
  coaching that raises their income

Any site we build should serve the business owner first, with a clear secondary
path for reps who want to join the bench.
